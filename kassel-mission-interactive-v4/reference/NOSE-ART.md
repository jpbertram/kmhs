# Nose art of the 35 Kassel Mission ships (27 September 1944)

Research note, 18 November 2026. No code or textures were changed. The Kassel Mission Historical Society holds no nose art images for these ships, so everything below comes from outside sources.

## How to read this

- **Documented** means a source names the ship by serial and states the fact. **Inferred** means it is my reading of incomplete evidence.
- "Name known" is not the same as "art known". 8th Air Force Liberators that had a name usually carried it painted on the nose, often with a figure, but the lettering style and any figure are unknown until a photo is checked.
- "Nothing documented" means no source found gives the ship a name or nose art. The best default for the simulator is a plain nose with only the standard markings (inferred). It is not proof the nose was bare.
- No images are stored in the repo. The photos found sit on the American Air Museum in Britain (IWM) site with IWM, NARA or private-collection credits, and this environment cannot download from that host. Links are given instead. NARA photos are public domain and are the safest to copy later.

## Gaps in the simulator roster (`km3d-v3.js`, `V2.ROSTER`)

These are notes for whoever paints the noses. Nothing was changed. JP approved these fixes on 18 Nov 2026, to be made after that night's release has merged.

- `nick:'Uncle'` on Heitz should be "Bugs Bunny". "Uncle" is the call sign.
- `nick:'Maria Lupine'` on Smith may be anachronistic for September 1944 (see group 2).
- Named ships with no `nick` in the simulator: Hunter, French, Hansen, Walther, Swofford, Reynolds, Bruland, Jones, Sollien, Johnson, Warman, Elder, Mowat.
