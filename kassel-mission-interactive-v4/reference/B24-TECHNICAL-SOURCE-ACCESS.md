- AirCorps catalog identifies the **15 August 1944 AN 01-5EE-1** flight operating instructions covering B-24G/H/J: https://app.aircorpslibrary.com/aircraft/consolidated-b-24-liberator. Catalog content is not the manual itself; membership access has not been supplied. Do not substitute the locally available B-24D manual or B-24G test report as proof of J-specific configuration.
- Museum of Flight B-24 pilot training manual catalog lead: https://digitalcollections.museumofflight.org/nodes/view/2825. Browser fetch returned 403; content not inspected. No claim about exact pages, dimensions or diagrams follows from the listing.
- B-24 Liberator Restoration Australia flight-manual holdings catalog: https://www.b24australia.org.au/library/air-craft-manuals-b-24/flight-manuals. Full catalog inspected. It lists an original hardback B-24D/J manual dated January 1944, plus AN 01-5EE-1 B-24G/H/J/PB4Y-1 instructions dated January 1944 and February 1944 revision. These are pre-mission-date holdings and a concrete alternative to the membership catalog. Dates and titles are catalog metadata, not independently verified title pages. The page provides no digital manual attachment; no request sent. A targeted Victorian Collections search did not locate a matching digitized manual. Do not count the unrelated search-result PDF as aircraft evidence.

## Required evidence remains missing

For schematic-exact interiors: aircraft manufacturing block/plant, station coordinates, compartment and floor sections, cockpit/turret/exit assembly drawings and mission-specific equipment layout. For aerodynamic validation: applicable flight conditions, weight/CG, inertia, propulsion and control data, stability/control coefficients and independent reference cases spanning the maneuvers/damage states actually rendered. A generic drawing catalog and steady-level G-model test points cannot certify those requirements.

The runtime remains a source-constrained visual reconstruction with numerical continuity tests, not a validated B-24J aerodynamic simulation. Keep this boundary explicit while acquiring stronger evidence.
