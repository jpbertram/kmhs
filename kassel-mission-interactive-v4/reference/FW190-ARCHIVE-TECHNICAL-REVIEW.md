# Fw 190 archive technical review

Entire scanned pages 88 and 91 inspected from `Germans and Germany/Luftwaffe/Planes/FW-190A/Schematic/`. Filenames say February 1975; the page-88 printed footer says **February 1976**. Preserve that bibliographic discrepancy. These are later magazine illustrations, not factory drawings.

Page 88 explicitly identifies its cutaway as **Fw 190A-8**. The illustrated equipment includes a BMW 801D-2 fourteen-cylinder radial engine, VDM three-blade adjustable-pitch constant-speed propeller, annular oil tank/cooler and twelve-blade cooling fan. Armor and canopy details are keyed separately. The drawing also depicts optional WfrGr 21 equipment; its appearance does not prove that Kassel's attacking aircraft carried it. Do not apply every accessory to every aircraft or treat the illustration as a variant-specific A-8/R2 operational configuration certificate.

Page 91 provides a keyed A-8 cockpit instrument layout, separating airspeed, attitude, vertical speed, compass, boost, tachometer, fuel/oil and propeller-pitch indications. This can guide a future fighter cockpit reconstruction; it is not a B-24 interior source.

The surrounding performance discussion includes captured **A-3** comparisons and an **A-4/U8** test description. Those data cannot validate the imported A-8 model or armored Sturm configuration. No aerodynamic coefficient tables, mass/inertia model or flight-test uncertainty sufficient for a validated Kassel fighter simulation were established from these pages. Avoid promoting an attractive cutaway into aerodynamic validation.

Next required model audit: distinguish propeller from cooling fan and verify A-8/R2 armament/armor against aircraft-specific mission evidence; preserve the correct native propeller axis already tested. Use illustrations as references, never as pasted cockpit skins.
