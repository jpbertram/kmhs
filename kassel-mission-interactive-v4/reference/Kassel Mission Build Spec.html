Kassel Mission Interactive — Full-Mission 3D Experience

Spec date 14 Aug 2026 · Status: awaiting "proceed" · No build work started. This document is the complete handoff — any competent implementer (or a future session) can build from it alone.

1 · Deliverable

A standalone, public-facing, browser-based 3D piece (desktop + phone) that plays the 445th Bomb Group's Kassel Mission of 27 September 1944 as one scrubbable timeline — 0800 takeoff from Tibenham to the ~1230 return window — compressed to 5 minutes, rendered in three.js with the real KMHS aircraft GLBs. The viewer can, at any time, pick any ship in the formation by crew name, then pick any of its 10 crew stations, and watch the mission first-person from that seat. Ships meet their documented fates; the battle over the Seulingswald plays automatically and historically from astern. Neutral documentary tone. Real names and serials.

Delivery is sliced: Slice 1 = battle sequence + crew/station system working end-to-end. Slice 2 = full mission timeline, route-map inset, ground scenes, audio.

4 · Historical basis

39 B-24s of the 445th BG (squadrons 700/701/702/703) were scheduled from Tibenham, Norfolk; 4 aborted, 35 bombed. Lead ship: Capt. John Chilton (703rd crew flying with the 700th), Maj. Donald McCoy command pilot; deputy lead Capt. Web Uebelhoer with Capt. Jim Graham. Weather: solid undercast, base ~3,000 ft, tops 6–7,000 ft; PFF drop planned. At the IP the lead turned almost due east instead of ESE — a ~70 mph tailwind put the group ~25 mi NE of Kassel at bomb release. Bombs fell near Göttingen (village of Rosdorf/Grone area). Radio warnings from other navigators were answered only with "Keep it tight — keep it together." Now ~100 mi behind the division and unescorted, the group turned west and, around 11:00, was hit over the Seulingswald (between Bad Hersfeld and Eisenach) by 100–150 fighters — Fw 190 Sturmböcke in line-abreast waves from astern, with Bf 109 cover — for roughly six minutes. 25 B-24s went down in or near the battle area; P-51s (incl. 361st FG; Lt. Leo Lamb was killed) arrived as the blow ended and chased the fighters off. Of ~10 aircraft still flying: French crash-landed at Metz-Frescaty (FR), Hunter east of Lille (FR), Heitz near Brussels (BE); Dewey and Mercer put down at Manston emergency strip; Krivik crash-landed at Old Buckenham; four landed back at Tibenham. Cost: 117 KIA (incl. 12 murdered on the ground), 121 POW. Luftwaffe: 29 fighters, 18 pilots lost. Chilton's lead ship was among the last lost; it crashed at Bad Hersfeld, where the 1990 memorial now stands.

7 · Crew & ship selection

Side panel, two levels: (1) Crew list — every rendered ship, listed as "Pilot surname · squadron · aircraft name/serial · fate"; (2) Station list for the selected ship — 10 stations with role captions. Breadcrumb back to crew list. Keyboard: ←/→ cycle stations, ↑/↓ cycle ships, Esc to orbit. Selecting a station triggers the lazy b24j.glb fetch (once, then cached template is cloned per ship).

The formation renders 9–12 ships; each maps to a documented aircraft. Roster (build task: finalize against kasselmission.org squadron pages / Kassel Mission Reports): Chilton/McCoy lead (703rd w/700th, PFF — shot down late, crashed Bad Hersfeld; default selected ship), Uebelhoer/Graham deputy lead (survivor — landed Tibenham), Schaen (702nd, high right — lost), Hautman "Mairzy Doats" (700th — lost), plus the documented straggler pilots as fates require: Dewey, Mercer (Manston), Krivik (Old Buckenham), French (Metz), Hunter (Lille), Heitz (Brussels), and 1–2 further documented losses to fill the box. Each roster entry: {slug, pilot, squadron, acName, serial, fate, fateTime, formationSlot} in a data JSON.

When the selected ship is lost: hit + fire at its documented point in the wave sequence, camera (if aboard) shakes, then exterior follow-cam tracks the ship down to the cloud deck, holds a beat, cuts to the survivors' formation. Panel then shows the ship's fate line and offers the surviving ships. Seeking backward on the scrubber restores everything (all state derives from timeline position — pure function of t, no accumulated state).

9 · Battle choreography (2:45–4:00)

  • Geometry: three waves from astern, line-abreast Fw 190s (6–9 rendered per wave, instanced), closing from ~1.5 km behind/below-level, firing pass through the box, break high; Bf 109s cross high cover. Camera never auto-cuts — passes are seen from wherever the viewer is.
  • Losses: scripted per roster ship at fixed t; sequence = hit flash → engine fire + black smoke ribbon (particle/sprite trail) → peel out of formation → falling arc to the cloud deck → gone. 3–5 parachutes blossom behind each loss (small sprites). Losses among non-rendered ships are conveyed by the caption counter, not faked geometry.
  • Own-ship hit (default ship ~3:20): cannon-strike flashes on the airframe, fire from #3, interior camera shake; controls stay live ~4 s; then follow-cam sequence (§7).
  • P-51 arrival ~3:50: 4–6 Mustangs dive through from high 6 o'clock, fighters withdraw east. Caption notes the escort's late arrival and Lt. Leo Lamb (361st FG), killed in the defense.
  • Restraint: no gore, no audio screams, no slow-motion glamour shots. Fire/smoke are documentary, not spectacle.

10 · Presentation layer

  • Style source: inspect the live staging viewer at build time and match its sky/haze treatment, overlay typography, and control styling. The attached design-system project is empty — the staging page is the binding reference.
  • Header: "The Kassel Mission — 27 September 1944" + subtitle "445th Bombardment Group · Mission 169". Sources/credit line in a footer (kasselmission.org, 445bg.org, Kassel Mission Reports).
  • Clock/caption bar: mission time (24-h), phase caption, loss counter during battle. Units shown dual: "22,000 ft (6,700 m)".
  • Scrubber: full-width bottom bar; phase tick marks + labels; play/pause; current mission time bubble. Position persisted to localStorage and restored on load.
  • Map inset (corner, collapsible on phone): real-geography route Tibenham → coast → IP → Göttingen → Seulingswald → return legs incl. straggler endpoints; intended-vs-actual track shown from the IP; dot synced to t. Built from real geo data per the maps recipe.
  • Overcast: volumetric-look cloud deck (layered noise sprites or shader plane) hiding the ground from altitude; terrain imagery visible only in ground phases (takeoff/landing) and below cloud breaks in the fall sequence.
  • Audio: WebAudio-synthesized engine drone (layered detuned saws + LFO), distant gunfire/cannon thumps during battle; starts muted with a visible unmute control; volume ducks under captions. No recorded-audio assets.

11 · Loading & performance

  • Startup: fetch km-b24-olive (+ fighters + p51 deferred until first needed) → orbit scene playable fast. b24j.glb (8 MB) fetched only on first station entry, with an in-panel progress bar (measured 11.2 s cold in the Webflow context); ship stays selectable meanwhile.
  • Cloning: one loaded template per GLB, cloned per aircraft (mirrors the km3d-v2 templates pattern — the one injection point that worked, per D3).
  • Budget: formation of 9–12 olive clones + fighters ≪ the viewer's 4.25 M-tri baseline; detailed b24j swapped in only for the ship the camera occupies (one instance at a time — 174 k tris).
  • D4 lesson: single renderer for the page lifetime; dispose geometries/materials/textures and the context on teardown; cap pixel ratio at 2 (1.5 on phone); no re-init loops.
  • Phone: reduced clone count (7), no shadows, panel collapses to a bottom sheet, hit targets ≥44 px.
  • CORS: if any CDN fetch fails cross-origin, halt with a visible notice and ask the user to upload the GLBs to the project. No placeholder geometry.

13 · Open items & risks

  • Station blurbs (§8), timeline captions (§6), and the final ship roster (§7) are drafts — user review requested during Slice 1.
  • Exact IP / bombs-away / landing times need the 445th mission report; approximations are flagged and isolated in TIMELINE.
  • CDN CORS behavior unverified from this environment — first build step is a fetch probe; on failure, request uploads (locked fallback).
  • b24j interior detail level at nav/radio/ball stations unknown until loaded — some stations may need camera-offset nudges after first visual check.
  • This piece is standalone; it does not patch km3d-v2. The D1–D4 defects in the handoff brief remain open for the site's own refactor.
