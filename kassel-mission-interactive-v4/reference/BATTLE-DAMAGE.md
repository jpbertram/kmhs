# Battle damage by aircraft, 27 September 1944

What each ship shows in the simulator (aircraft-damage-data.js). Source: the Society crew accounts in
`society-crew-cms-snapshot.json`. **documented** = the account says it; **engine inferred** = the account says it but not which engine, so one is chosen;
**inferred** = deduced, with the reason. The accounts give no clock times, so *when* each item appears is always inferred from the roster cue.

## Ball turrets

No ship is shown with a ball turret. Documented removed: Chilton (H2X radar dome in the well), Smith, Sollien. Every other ship: inferred from the Smith account ("With the Liberators’ ball turrets recently removed") and from the participant records, where the only ball gunner listed (McCormick) flew on Chilton’s radar ship.

## Damage

### Chilton
- **fuselage holes (bombbay)**, documented. "They filled our bomb-bay with 20 & 30 mm cannons"
- **fuselage fire**, inferred. "The leading Liberator, on fire from nose to tail" Quote is from the Hunter crew story (Spera, lead element of the lead squadron); identifying "the leading Liberator" as Chilton's ship is an inference, supported by Uebelhoer seeing the lead ship glow "an orange appearance".
- **explosion**, documented. "The crew members on the flight deck were in the airplane when it exploded."

### Uebelhoer
- **wing hole No. 2 (left wing)**, documented. "a single projectile through the No. 2 engine’s self-sealing oil reservoir"

### Hunter
- **fuselage holes (bombbay)**, documented. "A 20-millimeter shell tore through the bomb bay, ripping off the doors and severing fuel lines."
- **bombbay fire (bombbay)**, documented. "Two fires started simultaneously in the bay."
- **wing fuel leak (bombbay)**, documented. "fuel was pouring out like water from a fire hydrant"
- **fuselage holes (waist)**, documented. "A bullet through the waist"
- **engine dead No. 3 (right wing)**, engine inferred. "With one engine gone and a second quitting on approach" The record does not give the engine number; shown on No. 3.
- **engine dead No. 2 (left wing)**, engine inferred. "a second quitting on approach" The record does not give the engine number; shown on No. 2.

### French

- **fin rudder damage (fin)**, documented. "the right rudder shot away"
- **bombbay fire (bombbay)**, documented. "a 20mm shell tore off the bomb-bay doors and started two fires over the fuel tanks"
- **engine dead No. 3 (right wing)**, inferred. "nursed the shuddering bomber west on two and three engines" "Two and three engines" implies a second engine besides No. 1 was intermittently lost; number not recorded. The record does not give the engine number; shown on No. 3.

### Bruce

- **engine fire No. 1 (left wing)**, inferred.  The simulator's existing loss note says Nos. 1 and 2 engines were afire; that source is not in the Society crew record.
- **engine fire No. 2 (left wing)**, inferred.  The simulator's existing loss note says Nos. 1 and 2 engines were afire; that source is not in the Society crew record.

### Golden

### Swofford

### Reynolds

- **hydraulics out**, documented. "oxygen, hydraulics, radio and intercom shot out"

- **interphone out**, documented. "oxygen, hydraulics, radio and intercom shot out"
