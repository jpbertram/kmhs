# Bundled aircraft models

- `km-p51-361st.glb`: "P-51 Mustang" by UlissesVinicios
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/p-51-mustang-36f0f3e71d2a4c18b479db1ae8f9e7a7),
  licensed CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Converted from the supplied scene.gltf, scene.bin and textures into one
  GLB; textures resized (colour and metal/roughness 1024 px JPEG, normals
  512 px PNG). Geometry unchanged.
  Livery: the supplied textures carried a stock "Noise Boy" scheme. For the
  Kassel rescue the red nose and spinner were recoloured to the 361st Fighter
  Group's chrome yellow, the red tail to natural metal, and the "Noise Boy"
  name and B8 codes painted out. Stars, bars and stripes are unchanged.
